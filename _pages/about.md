I am **Yiqian Gong (龚易乾, CodeAlan)**, an M.Eng. student in Electronic Information at the **Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech)** in Shenzhen. I am currently part of the SUSTech National Graduate College for Engineers.

My work focuses on **embodied AI and robotics**, especially:

- Vision-Language Navigation (VLN)
- Reinforcement learning and imitation learning
- Large language models for robotics and navigation
- Multi-modal perception, SLAM, and autonomous navigation

I previously studied Communication Engineering at **South China Normal University (SCNU)**, where I co-founded and served as Vice Captain and Vision Group Leader of the VANGUARD RoboMaster team. I enjoy turning research ideas into complete, reproducible systems in C/C++, Python, and ROS.

## Education and experience

- **2025.09 - present:** M.Eng. in Electronic Information, SUSTech National Graduate College for Engineers.
- **2021.09 - 2025.06:** B.Eng. in Communication Engineering, South China Normal University.
- **2022.09 - 2024.06:** Co-founder and Vice Captain, VANGUARD RoboMaster Team.

## Selected engineering work

- **Multi-sensor fusion SLAM for UAV systems** - designed a PX4, MAVROS, Gazebo, and QGroundControl simulation and deployment workflow, including odometry, navigation, and decision modules.
- **RoboMaster autonomous systems** - worked on auto-aiming, ballistic calculation, sentry navigation, and robot decision-making.
- **Outdoor ROS vehicle race** - contributed to cone detection, 2D LiDAR mapping, vision-LiDAR fusion, and TEB-based trajectory planning. The team won National First Prize (5th nationwide) in the 18th competition.
