I am **Yiqian Gong (龚易乾)**, an M.Eng. student in Electronic Information at the **Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech)** in Shenzhen.

My work focuses on **robotics and embodied intelligence**, especially:

- Vision-Language Navigation (VLN)
- Robot perception, SLAM, and autonomous navigation
- Reinforcement learning and large language models for robotics
- RoboMaster and intelligent vehicle systems

I previously studied Communication Engineering at **South China Normal University (SCNU)** and worked with the VANGUARD RoboMaster team. I enjoy turning research ideas into complete, reproducible systems in C/C++, Python, and ROS.

## Selected open-source projects

- [SolveTrajectory](https://github.com/CodeAlanqian/SolveTrajectory) - ballistic trajectory calculation with an air-resistance model.
- [2023ROS_Autocar](https://github.com/CodeAlanqian/2023ROS_Autocar) - first-prize national competition code for an outdoor ROS autonomous vehicle.
- [SCNU-RM-Sentry](https://github.com/CodeAlanqian/SCNU-RM-Sentry) - mapping and navigation code for a RoboMaster sentry.
- [SNN-MNIST](https://github.com/CodeAlanqian/SNN-MNIST) - spiking neural network experiments with PyTorch and SpikingJelly.

For code, notes, and more projects, visit [GitHub](https://github.com/CodeAlanqian). You can also read my blog at [codealan.top](https://codealan.top).
